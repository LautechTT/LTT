Who We Are

Built
Together.

LAUTECH Smashers is the table tennis team representing Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH).

Founded in 2024, the team brings together passionate and dedicated table tennis players with a shared commitment to excellence, teamwork, discipline, and competitive growth.

2024
Our Story

The journey
began.

LAUTECH Smashers was established in 2024 to provide a platform for table tennis players at LAUTECH to develop their skills, compete at a high level, and proudly represent their university.

Since its formation, the team has continued to grow through training, competition, and shared experiences. Every match and training session is an opportunity for our players to improve, challenge themselves, and contribute to the success of the team.

Our Achievements

Making our
mark.

Since 2024, LAUTECH Smashers has made its mark in various table tennis competitions and tournaments. The team has won medals at different competitions, including participation in major university sporting events such as the Nigeria University Games (NUGA).
