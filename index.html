MORE THAN A GAME.

LAUTECH Smashers is the table tennis team representing Ladoke Akintola University of Technology, Ogbomoso, Oyo State.

Built around discipline, teamwork, determination and continuous improvement, the team brings together students who share a passion for the sport.
